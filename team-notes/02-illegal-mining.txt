ILLEGAL MINING & ENCROACHMENT EARLY WARNING - THE TEAM EXPLAINER
(Everything you need to explain this idea in 2 minutes)


THE ONE-LINER
We watch the ground from space, and when someone starts digging where they
shouldn't, we notice within days and alert the authorities automatically.

WHY IT MATTERS
Illegal mining is hard to catch - inspectors can't be everywhere, and by
the time anyone visits, the damage is done. Rivers are polluted, forests
are stripped, and government land is quietly taken. Our system makes the
sky the inspector.

HOW IT WORKS - THE ANALOGY
Imagine photographing your garden every week. One week a patch is grass,
the next week it's a hole. You don't need to be an expert to spot the
difference - you just compare the two photos. We do this with satellite
images of the whole state. When new digging appears, we measure how big it
is, check what's nearby (a river? a forest? a protected zone?), and check
whether the digging is allowed by a permit. All of this is scored 0-100 -
the higher the score, the more urgent the alert.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING (simple pipeline)
1. Satellite passes every 5-12 days, photos go into a storage lake.
2. Software cleans the images (removes clouds, corrects the view).
3. It computes "plant health" and "bare ground" signals from colors.
4. It compares the newest image to the previous one - new pits, exposed
   soil, access roads, trucks and excavators all get flagged.
5. A risk engine adds up the evidence: excavation size + tree loss +
   distance to river + overlap with protected land + no permit.
6. Score 70+ = instant SMS to the district officer with map location.

THE DEMO STORY
A judge watches a mining pit appear between two satellite dates on a map.
The system detects the new excavation, shows it's 85 m from a river and
inside a forest buffer, finds no permit, scores it 87/100 HIGH - and an
alert with location, area and evidence is generated for the officer.

TECH, DECODED
- Satellite images: free EU data (Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 optical).
- NDVI: a "greenness score" of the ground from the photo's colors.
- Change detection: subtracting old photo from new photo pixel by pixel.
- U-Net / YOLO: the brains - one outlines regions, one spots objects.
- Risk engine: a simple points system (like a driving license score).
- PostGIS: a map-aware database that answers "what's near this spot?"

KEY TALKING POINTS (say these with confidence)
1. "Satellites catch digging within days - before inspectors can."
2. "Multiple checks: area, vegetation loss, river/forest proximity,
   permits - combined into one risk score out of 100."
3. "Legal mining with a valid permit is automatically not flagged."
4. "If no AI model is trained yet, the color-difference method still
   works - the demo never depends on unavailable data."
