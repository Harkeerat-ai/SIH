CROP HEALTH & MICRO-INSURANCE ENGINE - THE TEAM EXPLAINER
(Everything you need to explain this idea in 2 minutes)


THE ONE-LINER
Every farm plot gets a satellite health check every week, and when a crop
fails, the system builds an insurance claim's evidence automatically - but
a human always approves the payment.

WHY IT MATTERS
Millions of farmers rely on crop insurance, but claims take months because
someone must visit each damaged field to verify it. That's slow, costly
and open to fraud. Our system verifies damage from space in days, and
produces an evidence file insurers can trust.

HOW IT WORKS - THE ANALOGY
A doctor checks a child's height against a growth chart: "expected for
your age" vs "actual". If the child is suddenly far below the line, the
doctor investigates. We do this for crops. Satellites measure how green
and healthy a plot is each week, we compare it to the expected growth
curve for that crop in that district, and when the curve suddenly crashes
below normal, we estimate how much yield was lost. The farmer just submits
a claim - the satellite evidence is already waiting.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING (simple pipeline)
1. Every farm plot becomes a shape on a map, linked to its farmer,
   crop and insurance policy.
2. Every 5 days a satellite passes; we compute each plot's greenness
   score (cloud-free pixels only - no guessing through clouds).
3. We compare to the expected curve built from 3+ years of history.
4. A crash for two checkups in a row = damage detected; an ML model
   estimates damage probability and yield loss %.
5. A rules engine turns this into a recommendation:
   auto-reject (tiny loss) / field verification (usual) / approve.
6. The evidence package (health before, health after, loss %, weather
   data, image count, confidence) goes to the insurer - human authority
   signs off on every payout.

THE DEMO STORY
A judge opens a soybean plot: health 82% expected, 46% observed, estimated
loss 44%, rainfall and moisture anomalies confirmed. The system generates
a ready-to-send claim verification report with 12 satellite images as
evidence - and marks it "field verification required" because a human must
still confirm.

TECH, DECODED
- NDVI/NDMI: greenness and moisture scores from the satellite photo.
- Baseline engine: the "growth chart" built from years of history.
- XGBoost: a fast, explainable machine-learning model (trees that vote).
- Rules engine: if/then insurance logic, not the AI, decides payouts.
- Evidence package: the claim's proof folder, auto-generated.
- TimescaleDB: a database built for time-series data like weekly scores.

2. "We compare each plot to its own expected growth curve - we don't

3. "The AI estimates damage; the rules engine recommends; a human

4. "Farmers see one simple screen: my crop health, my risk, my claim."
